## TuneFriend v2.44

### Playback reliability (wake / Doze)
- Wi‑Fi lock + faster stall recovery so music is less likely to die with the screen off
- Position-frozen streams reload automatically
- Screen unlock and opening the app re-kick playback immediately

### Home screen widgets
- **Now Playing** — title, artist, prev / play-pause / next, **thumbs up/down**
- **Mini Player** — compact transport
- **Shuffle Liked** — one-tap shuffle of 👍 songs

### Playlists
- Long-press (or right-click) an **album** → add all tracks to a playlist (or create one)

### Notes
- Widget 👍/👎 sync into the app when you next open it
- Keep **Battery → Unrestricted** for best overnight play
