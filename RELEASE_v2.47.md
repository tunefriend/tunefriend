## TuneFriend v2.47

### Audio focus (calls / YouTube / notifications)
- Do **not** restart music over an active **phone call**
- Do **not** steal audio back from **YouTube** after a notification
- Permanent focus loss (another app) no longer auto-resumes TuneFriend
- Transient loss (calls) still resumes when the call ends
- Health checks / screen-on no longer re-request audio focus while paused for another app

### Shuffle skips blocked tracks (GitHub #6)
- **Shuffle All** and **Liked → Shuffle/Play** exclude thumbs-down songs and blocked artists
- Queue auto-advance also skips blocked tracks
- Android Auto / widget liked sync excludes blocked tracks

### Also
- Target Android 16 (API 36)
- Offline downloads + Downloads tab
